# E-commerce Data Analysis Project

## Project Overview
Analyzed an e-commerce dataset containing 99,442 records to understand business performance, customer purchasing behavior, payment methods, and delivery trends. Used Excel, SQL, Power Query, Power BI, and Tableau for data cleaning, analysis, KPI calculation, and dashboard creation.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Excel & Power Query:** Cleaned and transformed 99,442 records and created dashboards for different business analyses.
- **SQL:** Imported datasets, analyzed business requirements, and calculated KPIs.
- **Power BI:** Developed the main interactive dashboard to track key business metrics and performance.
- **Tableau:** Created additional interactive dashboards for different types of business analysis.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Created 6 KPIs to track:
1. Total Revenue
2. Average Shipping Days
3. Total Orders
4. Average Price
5. Average Payment
6. 5-Star Credit Card Orders

## Key Charts & Analysis
Created 6 charts to understand different aspects of e-commerce business performance:
1. Total Payment by Weekdays and Weekends
2. Average Delivery Days
3. Order Status Distribution
4. Top 10 Product Categories by Revenue
5. Monthly Revenue Trend
6. Payment Method Distribution

## Interactive Filters
Added interactive slicers and filters for:
- Year
- Month
- Display
- Customer State
- Payment Type

## Dashboards
Created multiple dashboards in Excel, Power BI, and Tableau to perform different types of analysis. The main dashboard was developed in Power BI, with additional dashboards in Excel and Tableau to explore business performance from different perspectives.

## Project Objective
To understand e-commerce business performance, identify sales and delivery trends, analyze customer payment behavior, and provide useful business insights through interactive dashboards.
